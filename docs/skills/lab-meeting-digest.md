---
title: lab-meeting-digest
skill: lab-meeting-digest
status: brief
comments: true
recipe: 01-make-materials-accessible
check_level: expert
does: Reads the week's project updates and posts a summary on the morning before lab meeting.
inputs:
- 'The week''s project updates: notes, issues, commits or messages.'
- The list of projects and the people working on them.
outputs:
- A digest of overlaps between projects, overdue items and suggested priorities, with a link to each source.
steps:
- Collect the updates since the last meeting.
- Summarise each project in two or three lines.
- List overlaps between projects and items that are overdue.
- Suggest priorities, and mark them as suggestions.
- Post the digest at the scheduled time.
local:
- Where do project updates live (repositories, issue tracker, notes tool, chat)?
- When is lab meeting, and when should the digest arrive?
- Where should the digest be posted, and who can read it?
- Which projects or people must be left out?
never:
- Never post anything about a person's performance.
- Never post to a channel outside the group.
check: Every line links to its source. The group leader reads the digest before the meeting.
test: Run it on last week's updates, and compare the digest with what was discussed in the meeting.
