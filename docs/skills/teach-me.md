---
title: teach-me
skill: teach-me
status: brief
comments: true
recipe: 09-write-review-teach
check_level: expert
does: Turns a topic or paper into an interactive tutorial with questions.
inputs:
- A topic, paper or chapter.
- The level of the learner.
outputs:
- A tutorial in steps, each with an explanation, a question and the answer.
- The sources for each step.
steps:
- Read the material and list the concepts it needs.
- Order them from basic to advanced.
- Write one step per concept, with a question that checks understanding.
- Cite the source for each step.
local:
- Who are the learners, and what do they already know?
- In which format should the tutorial be delivered (web page, notebook, flashcards)?
- Which tasks must students do by hand first?
never:
- Never present a claim without a source.
- Never replace a task the course requires students to do by hand.
check: The teacher works through the tutorial once before using it.
test: A tutorial on a topic you teach. Check every answer.
---
